# Changelog

## 1.2

- A Now Playing strip sits in the header while any app has a media session playing or paused: small cover, title, artist, a thin progress line, and Previous, Play or Pause, Next on the D-pad. It uses the colours and typefaces of the active scheme. Nothing is drawn when no session is active.
- The strip follows any app that publishes a media session (Apple Music Web, NetEase Cloud Music, Spotify, and others) and prefers the one that started playing last.
- The page does not dim while a session is playing, and the screen is kept awake. Paused or stopped, the idle dim comes back after the usual time.
- Settings, Now playing: a switch for the strip, and a button to the system notification-access screen. The access is granted once. Without it nothing is shown and nothing errors.

## 1.1.1

- Settings actions for an app (Pin, Unpin, Rename, Hide, Show, Move) run from the D-pad and from Enter.
- No default city and no default ticker symbols. A fresh install leaves both for you to set.

## 1.1

- Photographs advance every 5 seconds by default. Settings keeps 5 seconds, 10 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 5 minutes.
- The photo card no longer prints the file name.
- The focus marker stays under the name instead of cutting across the row.

## 1.0

First public build of Clean Launch, a D-pad home screen for Android TV.

- Three themes: Paper, Night Edition, and Late Edition.
- Weather uses a network location only when it matches the television, or a city set in Settings. An unknown city leaves the weather block empty.
- The ticker row stays off until symbols are added.
- Photographs are read from the app folder. An empty folder shows one CC0 sample, Fronalpstock.
- HDMI tiles follow the port number. AV and the tuner stay hidden until Settings shows them.
- No bundled site shortcuts beyond a plain X that opens https://x.com.
